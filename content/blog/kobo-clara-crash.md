## Kobo Clara crashes with a sideloaded book

I bought a new Kobo Clara Color, and soon realized that:

1. The white background on a color display is significantly darker than on a
   black-and-white display.
1. The white frame blinds me when I'm trying to read in the sun (which was one
   of the main purposes of having an ebook reader rather than reading from the
   phone)

Not sure what to do now. But I wanted to write about something else: the
crashes. There was a book I bought online, and unfortunately it came as a PDF
and there was no EPUB version available. I tried converting it using well known
tools but was dissatisfied with the conversion quality. Each line from the PDF
became a paragraph in the EPUB, causing a very awkward on-screen layout. So I
extracted the text from the PDF and made my own EPUB file, ran it through
kepubify for performance, and sideloaded to my Kobo Clara. I opened the ebook,
tapped the TOC, tapped the first chapter and the Clara reader crashed.
Specifically it displayed "Loading..." for a time, and then the book would
disappear and I'd find myself back in the main menu.
