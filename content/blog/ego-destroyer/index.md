When digital photograpy became widespread, everyone became a better
photographer.
<!--more-->
By the time you got the analog photos developed and brought the prints home, you
no longer remembered what you did. What were the light conditions? What was the
aperture or exposure time? Why did you pick this particular frame?

Take a photo, take a quick look, discover a problem, retake photo, confirm, done.
Everybody has done this so many times that we don't even think about how
wonderful it is.
