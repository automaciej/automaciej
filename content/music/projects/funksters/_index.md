A Warsaw funk and jazz band. The project grew out of weekly Tuesday jam sessions at the Oczko club in the Ochota Cultural Centre. The band toured the Baltic coast and performed twice in Denmark, recording original songs "Burza" and "Ocean" at Backyard Studio in Aarhus. In 2001 the band held a concert residency at the Soma club in Warsaw.
