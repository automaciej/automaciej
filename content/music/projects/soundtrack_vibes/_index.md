Soundtrack Vibes is an artistic collaboration between members of the NCH Gamelan Orchestra and Guests, creating a fusion of Western and Indonesian sounds. The performance will present classic TV themes adapted and arranged into complete compositions. Featuring new arrangements of music from Twin Peaks, Stranger things, Mad Men, White Lotus and more.

Members of the NCH Gamelan Orchestra will be joined on stage by guests musicians: Maciej Blizinski, David Rieger and Kim William Tyther.
