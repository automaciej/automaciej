"Level Design" is the debut album from Dublin-based jazz-funk quartet 8-bit
Funk, led by bassist, arranger and producer Leon Laisne. It reimagines iconic
video game themes through jazz, funk, fusion, neo-soul and electro-rock.

Maciej mixed the album.
