A warm, live-band reinterpretation of Naughty Boy's 2015 hit, featuring Beyoncé and Arrow Benjamin on the original. Recorded in the same Dublin session as 'River,' this version opens with clean guitar and Gaia's voice alone, building through Fender Precision slap bass, live drums from Riccardo Marenghi, and layered synths to a wall-of-sound chorus before dissolving into three-part backing vocal harmonies.
