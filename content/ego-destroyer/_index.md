_"Am I actually playing in time?"_

Your music teacher told you, "practice with a metronome". They weren't wrong,
but unless you're already good at hearing time, the metronome is mostly just
making noise in the same room as you.

<!--more-->

You need a measuring device. Ego Destroyer Metronome shows you your timing as
you play.

- Why does it destroy your ego?

  It shows you your timing, no softening. If you rush, you see it. If you drag,
  you see it. It might not be what you expect.

- Why would you want your ego destroyed?

  It will happen anyway. When you go to a recording studio or try to record
  yourself at home.
